Happy Glen Mapleworks

A different way of
thinking about maple.

Happy Glen starts with 440 acres of Vermont forest and a belief that life is multi-dimensional. Career executives and casual outdoorsmen. Global citizens and homebodies. High standards and sweaty messes.

Happy Glen is where conflicting parts come together as new connections. We start with Vermont maple and create products that reflect the way we live: thoughtfully made, connected to our experiences, and meant to be shared.

Our Story
01
What We Make Simply, we make maple products we actually want to cook with, drink, or put on our own table. Each product must pass our internal standard — are we proud of the quality such that we can share the result with our professional colleagues?
02
How We Make It At work and at Happy Glen we believe that simplicity breeds quality. We prioritize ingredients that are pure and straightforward, we execute craftsmanship without fuss, and prioritize authenticity over scale. The goal is not to make the most. It is to make something worth making.
03
Who We Make It With Happy Glen is about connection. We create products and stories to share with family, friends, colleagues, and the people we meet along the way — some in Vermont, some in Indiana, some scattered around the world. The people who become part of Happy Glen matter as much as the products themselves.

Why Happy Glen Exists

Happy Glen is not
an escape. It is a connection point.

Happy Glen started with a simple question: how do we keep making space for the aspects of our lives that make us who we are?

Our professional lives have always challenged us to learn, solve problems, and create impact. Happy Glen allows us to apply that same mindset outside of the office as our unpolished selves.

The forest is at the center of Happy Glen. It is our favorite spot to think clearly and have the conversations that matter most. Maple is a natural starting point because it comes directly from our happy place and requires us to put our capabilities to practical use.

Happy Glen is intentionally small. We are not trying to build the biggest maple company. The bigger idea is creating something worth working on and sharing with the people who are part of our lives.

The Founders

Phil & Sarah

We both grew up connected to Indiana and met while studying Chemical Engineering at Rose-Hulman then Strategic Management at IU. (Despite our degrees, we're Purdue fans. Boiler up!)

Our careers took us around the world, where we had the opportunity to work with talented people, solve difficult problems, and lead teams through complex challenges. Those experiences continued to shape how we approach everything: understand the problem and system, keep learning, and never stop trying to improve. We're engineers and problem solvers through-and-through.

Outside of work, we can be found in hiking boots, running shoes, or covered in flour. We spend our time exploring new places, cooking, being outside, and carrying the dog home from the coffee shop.

We are who we are and Happy Glen blends the aspects of our lives that were feeling in tension. Our professional lives were taking over. Now they enable a project that allows us to share back syrup, absurd stories of trial and mainly error, and a connection with the place we're choosing to call home.

The Land

Our original property (the farm) is 330 acres stretching approximately 1.5 miles end-to-end, and 700 feet of elevation change from the river to mountain peak. Happy Glen is essentially an entire side of a mountain with more than 3,000 tapped maple trees, 2 sugar stands, and countless miles of future hiking trails to be created.

The second property adds another perspective on the Vermont landscape. The 110 acres includes meadow, wetlands, and forest, including two resident beavers that have made their home there. The property borders the Appalachian Trail, connecting Happy Glen to a much larger landscape of forests, mountains, and people who spend their lives exploring them. This will be our future base of operations.

Together, these places represent what drew us to Vermont in the first place: a landscape that is beautiful, complex, and worth caring for.

Hi, I'm Glen, named after my breed and now namesake of Happy Glen. This seems a bit confusing actually but it makes sense … I am clearly the most important member of this and every operation.

I enjoy long walks in the forest (honestly, its the only place I actually walk), meeting new people, and making sure everyone knows I have arrived. I am admittedly stubborn and frequently difficult but adorably charming when I want to be.
