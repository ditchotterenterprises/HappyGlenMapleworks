Bourbon Barrel-Aged

The forest does more than make maple.

We're sustainably managing 440 acres and preserving the integrity of the forest through carbon capture while working to evolve operations into a net negative endeavor. Across 330 acres in Braintree and 110 acres in Hartford–Norwich, we're enrolled in Vermont's Current Use program and actively participating in the voluntary carbon credit market. This place was here long before us. We intend to leave it better.

Phil & Sarah · Founders

Pharma executives with maple-farming alter egos

The story starts at Rose-Hulman, where we met as Chemical Engineering students — small-town Indiana meets Navy brat. We've spent nearly 25 years in the pharmaceutical industry launching products, solving complex problems, and leading global teams. Even though the work is primarily done in the manufacturing plant, our best thinking always comes in the forest.

Along the way, we've had more adventures than we can count ... living as expats in China and Ireland, expedition mountain climbing, 75+ countries visited, and a lot of Macklemore concerts. We enjoy experiencing what makes people and places special, especially on foot and in the kitchen.

Now we're channeling our love for Vermont, our passion for hiking, Phil's love for cooking (mine for eating), and our skills as engineers into a new adventure: maple syrup! Our hope for Happy Glen is that we can connect our global community with a place we love. Welcome to Happy Glen.

Glen · Chief Enthusiasm Officer

Born in County Longford, Ireland. Currently napping on the couch.

Hi, I'm the Glen of Happy Glen Mapleworks. I'm named after my breed (the Glen of Imaal Terrier) and no dog is as "Glen" as me.

I'm Irish and spent my early days in Kinsale running around Dock Beach. Now I call the US and England home. I firmly believe everyone should know me which is why I frequent cafes, bars, and anywhere else where people may not have had the privilege of meeting me.

Hiking, wrestling with other big dogs, snarfing "food" off sidewalks, and long couch naps are my favorite things. I'm also delightfully stubborn — it's part of my charm. I bring enthusiastic disinterest and happiness to everything we do. Nice to meet you.
