Our Story

Engineers.
Explorers.
Sugarmakers.

The story starts at Rose-Hulman, where we met as Chemical Engineering students. We came from different backgrounds but we both believed in working hard and enjoying life along the way. Nearly 25 years later, we're still living that intentional life we set out on... 3 Dogs, 7 Continents, 8 Years as Expats, 75+ Countries, and more miles hiked and mountains climbed then we can count.

Along our adventures and careers in Pharma solving complex problems, we developed percerverance and an appreciation for the learning that comes with new experiences. Vermont is the place for us that feels like home with a sense of awe each time.

Now we're channeling our engineering knowledge, strategic thinking, and life experience into our own land. Our hope is that we can sustain this place and connect the people and places we've experienced with the location we've chosen as home. Welcome to Happy Glen.

Our Pillars

What we do

Barrel-Aged & Infusions

Maple that earns its place as an ingredient.

We barrel-age Vermont maple in bourbon barrels and develop infused expressions without additives or extracts. These are syrups built for the table, the chef's pantry, and the cocktail program.

Wholesale Sourcing

Grade A bulk supply for brewers and producers.

We supply bulk maple syrup to craft breweries, producers, and specialty buyers who care about provenance. We offer a direct relationship with the people who own the land.

Land Stewardship

440 acres managed for the long haul.

Across 330 acres in Braintree and 110 acres in Hartford–Norwich, we're enrolled in Vermont's Current Use program and long-term carbon capture. We are reinvesting in the forest so it keeps producing and thriving well beyond us.

Barrel-Aged

Bourbon Barrel-Aged

Vermont maple syrup aged in bourbon barrels. The oak and spirit notes deepen the maple character, making the result richer and more complex than syrup straight from the evaporator. Exceptional on its own, and a serious ingredient in cocktails, glazes, and anything that benefits from a little patience.

The forest does more than make maple.
